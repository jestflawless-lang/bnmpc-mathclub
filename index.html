A little about us

Why join our club?

BNPMC Math Club brings students together to discover the fun, challenge and beauty of mathematics—inside and beyond the classroom.

✳

Olympiad preparation

Practice for mathematical competitions with interesting problems and supportive feedback.

⌘

New perspectives

Explore number theory, combinatorics, geometry, logic and ideas beyond the usual syllabus.

✎

Community

Share approaches, learn from other students and celebrate every breakthrough.

Our purpose

Make room for wonder.

We aim to make mathematical thinking welcoming, collaborative and exciting. Whether you're new to puzzles or preparing for an olympiad, there is always something to learn.

  • Preparation through regular practice
  • Resources for advanced mathematical topics
  • Recognition for effort, creativity and achievement

How to play

  1. Press Start Game to begin the 60-second round.
  2. Solve each addition, subtraction, multiplication or division question.
  3. Submit your answer to score a point and get the next question.
  4. Try again to beat your best score this session.

This game runs in your browser. Scores are not uploaded or stored on a server.
